Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Hyper Kidz Reports ("the app") is an internal reporting tool operated by Hyper Kidz Dallas for its own management. It is not available to the public.

Google user data

The app requests a single Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send, and uses it only to send the venue's daily operations report e-mails from the venue's own Gmail account to the recipients the venue configures. The app does not read, store, index or share any Gmail messages, contacts or other Google user data. The OAuth token that allows sending is stored only in the venue's own systems and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Business data

Report figures come from the venue's own booking (ROLLER) and staff scheduling (7shifts) accounts and are kept in the venue's own database for trend and forecast calculations. Reports contain staff names and business totals; they do not contain customer names. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Staff dashboard

The staff dashboard shows the same figures to venue managers who have an account. Its data file is encrypted, and the figures can only be read in the browser after signing in; the password never leaves the device.

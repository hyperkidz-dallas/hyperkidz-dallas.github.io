Hyper Kidz Reports

Hyper Kidz Reports is an internal tool used by Hyper Kidz Dallas, an indoor playground in Dallas, Texas. It prepares daily operations reports (guests, revenue and staffing) from the venue's own booking and scheduling systems and e-mails them to the venue's management.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up. Its only Google permission is to send e-mail from the venue's own Gmail account.

Venue managers with an account can see the same numbers on the staff dashboard.
